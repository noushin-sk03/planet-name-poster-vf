## 1. Scientific Data Sources

All planetary physical metrics (gravity, temperatures, length of day, orbital period) are sourced directly from:
* **NASA Planetary Fact Sheet (Metric)**: [https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/)
* **NASA Planetary Satellite Fact Sheet (Moons)**: [https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/joviansatfact.html](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/joviansatfact.html)
* **NASA Moon Fact Sheet**: [https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/moonfact.html](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/moonfact.html)
* **NASA Sun Fact Sheet**: [https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/sunfact.html](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/sunfact.html)
* **NASA Exoplanet Archive (IPAC / Caltech)**: [https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/)

**Data License**: NASA scientific data is in the **Public Domain** under United States copyright law (17 U.S.C. § 105).
